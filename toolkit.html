Typography

Consider Shape and Its Effect: Geometric vs. Organic

Typography is more than choosing a font that looks good. The shape of a typeface can change the personality of an entire design.

While organic typefaces have more variation and can feel handmade or playful, using shape intentionally can communicate a feeling before someone even reads the words.

More on Shape

Weight: Creating Visual Contrast

Weight is another way typography can create hierarchy and guide the viewer through a design. A bold or heavy typeface naturally draws more attention, while lighter weights can create a quieter visual presence.

Color

Using Color Psychology When Designing

Color can communicate emotion before a viewer even reads the content. Different colors can influence the mood and personality of a design, which is why I think color should be chosen intentionally rather than added as decoration at the end.

More on Mood & Color

The 60-30-10 Ratio

A balanced color palette does not mean every color needs to be used equally. The 60-30-10 rule divides a design into three visual proportions: 60% for the main color, 30% for a secondary color, and 10% for an accent color.
